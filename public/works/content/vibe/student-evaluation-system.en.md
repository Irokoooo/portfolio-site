## Motivation

At the end of my freshman spring semester, during the calculation of comprehensive assessments and GPA scores, I encountered an extremely tedious management pain point as a class committee member: students submitted activity proof materials in an incredibly scattered way, and the school's comprehensive quality assessment scoring criteria were complex and lengthy. Because everyone interpreted the rules differently, the scores each person calculated for themselves varied widely — leaving class committee members to spend enormous time and energy repeatedly collecting materials and manually verifying them. My instinct was that the comprehensive assessment is, at its core, a highly institutionalized, rules-based computation. This kind of fully rules-driven work should be handled by a system automatically, rather than relying on manual accounting. So I decided to use AI-assisted development to build a standardized "University Student Comprehensive Assessment System."

## Pain Points

When building this system from scratch using Vibe Coding, the main technical and logical bottlenecks I faced were:

1. **Converting complex scoring rules into code:** The school's assessment guidelines involved not just basic GPA calculations, but also an extremely intricate set of bonus points (e.g., national-level, provincial-level, and school-level awards carry different weights, and each category has strict score caps). If you fed all the rules to the AI at once, the generated calculation logic would often contain conflicts or miss edge cases, leading to inaccurate total scores.
2. **Structuring the flow of proof materials:** The system was not just a calculator — it also needed a complete material collection and review workflow. This required building a full front-to-back-end interactive interface: the student-facing side for uploading categorized proof screenshots or documents, and the class-committee-facing side for clearly approving or rejecting submissions. For a non-CS student, untangling this kind of business flow involving data binding and state transitions was genuinely difficult.

## Implementation

1. **Converting management rules into algorithmic Prompts:** To ensure absolute calculation accuracy, I abandoned the crude approach of asking the AI to "write a comprehensive assessment system" in one go. I first manually decomposed the school's *Assessment Guidelines* into a clear logic tree and mathematical conditional expressions, then fed the rules to the large language model module by module (e.g., the ethics module, the academic module, the arts and sports module). By using this "feed the AI clean mathematical boundaries" Prompt strategy, the AI generated accurate, error-free JavaScript scoring functions.
2. **Step-by-step construction of a dual-console business system:** When building the material flow system, I planned two parallel tracks: a "student console (submission and progress tracking)" and a "class committee console (approval and one-click tallying)." By guiding the AI to write form components and data dashboards one by one, I successfully connected the complete workflow of material upload, categorized storage, and status tagging (pending / approved / rejected) — completely replacing the previous chaotic mix of Excel spreadsheets and WeChat group collection.

## Iteration Notes

After subsequent UI polish and code optimization, I submitted this system to the university. It is currently being officially deployed into the "Smart Minzu" campus information system. This project — which started from a class-level pain point and made its way to school-wide deployment — gave me a deep appreciation for this: the core power of Vibe Coding lies in breaking down the barrier between "business requirements" and "technical implementation." When you, as a class committee member, have an extremely grounded understanding of a real management pain point, AI becomes the best execution tool — capable of translating your clear management thinking and business logic into a genuinely productivity-liberating, institutionalized system, at low cost and with precision.
